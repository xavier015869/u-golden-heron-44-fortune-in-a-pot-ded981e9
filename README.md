## What it does

`main.py` writes a single kind, funny fortune for a houseplant — one line
of at most 120 characters — to `/output/entry.txt`. The fortune is a
prediction about the plant on the windowsill: the morning sun picks its
leaves out of the whole windowsill, and its keeper beams with pride.

## How to run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

It writes the fortune to `/output/entry.txt` (UTF-8, one line, 120
characters or fewer).
